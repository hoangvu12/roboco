### 731697b66 (#498) Steer mailbox backpressure plus `command_drain_lock` (residual of the side-chats squash)
- **For users:** with a burst of steers (an MCP fan-out, or many queued clicks on a live run), upstream waits for room in the run's mailbox. Roboco's `try_send` fails once the 32-slot mailbox is full and returns `NotSteerable` (`sessions.rs:1522`) or `false` (`:1090`). The send then takes the fallback route instead of being delivered to the live turn. Upstream's comment on the change: "Saturation is backpressure, not a dead runtime. Waiting for room preserves the live process and every accepted message in a burst." This applies to all platforms and is a rare edge case.
- **What's missing:** `steer_tx.reserve().await` before `registry.while_update_clear(...)`, with `permit.send` inside the gate, at both steer sites in `crates/engine/src/sessions.rs`. A blocking reserve then needs upstream's `command_drain_lock: tokio::sync::Mutex<()>` on `ChatDocHandle` (upstream `doc_host.rs:586,4886,5201,5265`). Prompt commands (Run/Steer) serialize on it while Interrupt and input controls bypass, so a stalled provider can still be stopped. Roboco files: `crates/engine/src/sessions.rs`, `crates/engine/src/doc_host.rs` (drain loop and `execute_command`).
- **Dependencies:** none upstream. `977fac614` explicitly excluded it; take the current upstream/main shape, not the #498 one. No wire change.
- **Risks:** drain ordering and deadlocks against Roboco's own `drain_lock`, the Stop/`await_earlier_controls` machinery and the CLI-update gate. Port with upstream's tests (message_queue burst steer).
- **Effort:** S–M.

### ed3df8fb1 (#544) Boot transcript-salvage sweep opens every journaled chat
- **For users:** at every engine start, after 5 s, `spawn_transcript_salvage` calls `open()` on every host chat that has a non-empty run journal. `open()` loads the snapshot into memory, spawns `chat_task` and runs `evict_over_budget`. On a long history this means a startup memory, CPU and IO spike, and fresh handles are protected from eviction for 30 s (`EVICT_MIN_IDLE_MS`). Upstream's fix: inspect the stored snapshot plus pending updates with a raw `LoroDoc` and only `open()` when there is something to salvage. This matters on both desktop platforms and for headless engines.
- **What's missing:** the inspection-before-open prologue of `salvage_chat_transcript` (`crates/engine/src/doc_host.rs:929`), adapted to engine-local Roboco (no chat2 epoch or edge checks; keep the `drain_lock` and `Arc::strong_count` re-check before appending). Optionally add the persistent "recovery" retry (upstream `schedule_sync_job(..., "recovery")`). That needs a store table, so a simpler route is to skip retrying. Port the test `salvage_inspection_does_not_open_healthy_or_unrecoverable_chats`.
- **Alternative:** the sweep only rescues the pre-chat2 → chat2 lineage (`{chat_id}.pre-chat2` rollback copies). Roboco never had a chat2 cutover of its own, so check whether any Roboco install can hold such rollback snapshots. If none can, gating or deleting the sweep is the smaller fix.
- **Dependencies:** none. The rest of #544 is cloud: `crates/sync/src/budget.rs`, `sync_jobs.rs`, chat2 HTTP transports, edge nudges, the `ChatSyncState` pill and `sync_resources` RPC. Do not port those. No wire change.
- **Risks:** low. Make sure the inspection reads exactly what `open()` would load (snapshot plus pending outbox updates).
- **Effort:** S. Close wave-7 ticket 16 against this narrowed scope, and close ticket 13 (#552) as cloud/no-op.
